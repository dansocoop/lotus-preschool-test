Our Story

Rooted in Values,
Ready for the World

Lotus International Preschool was founded on the belief that early childhood is the most transformative period in a human life. Like a lotus flower that rises beautifully from still waters, every child carries within them an extraordinary potential waiting to bloom.

Our world-class curriculum blends Montessori-inspired learning, play-based discovery, and value-centred education — delivered in a warm, nurturing environment designed to make every child feel safe, celebrated, and capable.
