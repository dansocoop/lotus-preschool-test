Our Story

A Beautiful Beginning
for Lifelong Learning

At Lotus International Preschool, we believe childhood is precious, and early learning should be joyful, meaningful, and deeply nurturing. Every child deserves an environment where they feel safe, valued, inspired, and encouraged to explore the world around them with confidence and curiosity.

Located within the serene Lotus Portico campus in Jankipuram Extension, Lucknow, our preschool combines thoughtfully designed spaces, modern early years education, experiential learning, and strong values to create a truly enriching childhood experience.

Our Approach

Rooted in Values, Ready for the World

Our child-centric philosophy combines modern early years education with warmth, care, creativity, and meaningful experiences. We encourage children to question, imagine, express, collaborate, and explore while developing confidence, empathy, communication skills, and independence.

Ages 2–6

Campus Experience

Designed for Wonder

Our beautifully designed campus provides calm, inspiring, and engaging spaces where children feel encouraged to learn, explore, create, and grow. From nature-inspired classrooms and discovery zones to reading corners and outdoor play areas, every environment is thoughtfully crafted to support joyful learning.

Discovery & Joy

Discovery • Values • Exploration

Meaningful Learning Inspired by Wonder

Our curriculum is built around immersive monthly themes that encourage children to explore the world through curiosity, creativity, movement, nature, storytelling, and hands-on discovery.

From sensory experiences in Playgroup to inquiry-led learning in Senior KG, each program is thoughtfully designed to support emotional growth, communication, confidence, and school readiness through joyful experiential learning.

What We Offer

Programs Designed for Every
Stage of Early Childhood

Thoughtfully crafted programs that meet every child exactly where they are — and gently guide them to where they can be.

Age 2–3

Playgroup

A Gentle Beginning to Learning

A nurturing first step into learning — where children feel safe, confident, and curious. Through sensory play, music, movement, storytelling, and exploration, little ones begin developing communication, independence, and a love for discovery.

Learn More
Age 3–4

Nursery

Building Curiosity & Confidence

The Nursery program encourages children to explore, imagine, and express themselves through experiential, play-based learning. Hands-on activities, storytelling, art, and thematic exploration help children build language, social, emotional, and early academic skills — all in a joyful environment.

Learn More
Age 4–5

LKG

Encouraging Independent Thinking

Our LKG program helps children strengthen confidence, communication, creativity, and foundational literacy and numeracy skills. Through thematic learning, collaborative activities, and guided exploration, children develop curiosity, problem-solving abilities, and school readiness.

Learn More
Age 5–6

UKG

Preparing Children for the Future

The UKG program prepares children for a smooth transition into formal schooling through a balanced approach to academics, creativity, communication, and independent learning. Children grow into confident, curious, and capable learners — ready for the world.
